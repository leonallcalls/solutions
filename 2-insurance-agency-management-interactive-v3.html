How Does Insurance Agency Call Routing Work?

Insurance agency call routing is the process of directing live inbound consumer calls to a specific licensed agent based on rules the agency sets. Those rules typically account for the agent's licensed states, product lines, real-time availability, campaign assignment, and any call limits or budget caps the agency has applied. When a consumer calls, the platform checks the call against those conditions and connects it to the agent who matches, in real time.

In AllCalls.io, an agency sets those rules once and applies them across the downline. Routing, agent assignment, spend controls, and reporting all operate inside the same platform that generates and delivers the calls.

Frequently Asked Questions
About Managing an Agency on AllCalls.io

Can I manage multiple agents, agencies, downlines, budgets, and funding within a single account?

Yes. AllCalls.io supports multi-level agency structures in which leadership manages agents, sub-agencies, and downlines through a single administrative account. Depending on your account architecture, you can create individual user profiles, assign agents to active campaigns, set permissions by role, apply daily or weekly budget caps, and track balances in real time across the entire structure.

Can I monitor calls, listen live, coach agents, and review performance?

Yes. Depending on your account permissions, your administrative dashboard lets you review call recordings, monitor call outcomes, and track conversion data on a per-agent basis. Granular reporting shows which agents, call types, and campaigns are producing the strongest return, which gives you something specific to coach against.

How is AllCalls.io different from a traditional lead vendor, dialer, or CRM?

AllCalls.io sources, screens, routes, and reports on consumer demand with a single platform. A lead vendor hands off a file. A dialer places outbound calls. A CRM records what happened after the fact. Because acquisition, routing, billing, and performance data live in the same system here, your agent-level reporting comes from the platform that delivered the call rather than from a reporting layer sitting on top of someone else's traffic.

Can I choose which states, carriers, demographics, opportunities, or call types I receive?

Yes. Routing supports targeting by state licensing, insurance vertical, language, call type, real-time agent availability, and current carrier or product access. Keeping your agents' licenses and preferences up to date within the platform gives the routing system what it needs to match live callers to the right agent. Highly restrictive filters depend on the available traffic volume in that segment.

How does Agent Match work, and does it improve routing over time?

Agent Match routes each call to the agents most likely to be a good fit, using performance signals, availability, call type, state, language, and your campaign rules. As more data accumulates on your team, routing decisions get better informed, sending more calls to the agents who are available, compliant, and performing well on that call type.

How does the AllCalls.io wallet and billing system work?

Your agency funds a central account balance and draws against it as calls are delivered. Billing details show what was spent, on which call types, and by which agent, with balance updates in real time. Because buffer periods apply on qualified and timed lines, calls that end before the billable threshold are not charged.

Is there a minimum spend, contract length, or commitment required to use AllCalls.io?

No contract and no minimum volume commitment. You fund your account, pay for the calls you receive, and adjust or stop your call flow when you need to. Volume-based tiered pricing is available for agencies buying at scale, and the sales team can review your traffic to build a rate that fits your structure.

How does AllCalls.io help with compliance, CMS requirements, recordings, and record retention?

AllCalls.io supports your compliance process through call tracking, secure call recording, detailed reporting, dynamic routing rules, and campaign controls. Every call is logged with the date, time, traffic source, duration, receiving agent, and call type, and recordings are retained securely for 10 years. Agencies and agents remain responsible for meeting CMS guidelines, TCPA, state licensing, carrier rules, and disclosure requirements.

Can AllCalls.io integrate with my CRM, dialer, or other systems?

Often, yes. Common integrations include pushing call data into a CRM, tracking dispositions, syncing agent availability, connecting call records, and passing data into existing reporting. Supported options depend on the systems you run and the level of customization required, and the team can review your setup.

Can I white-label the AllCalls.io platform for my agency?

White-label options may be available depending on agency size, use case, and partnership structure. A white-label setup lets your agency deliver call routing, tracking, reporting, and agent management under your own brand while running on AllCalls.io infrastructure. Availability, pricing, and setup requirements are discussed directly with the AllCalls.io team.
