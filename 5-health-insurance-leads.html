Five Inputs Decide
Where Each Call Goes

Select a node to see how each input shapes routing.

Availability

Routing starts with who can answer. AllCalls reads agent availability and status, so a call goes to an agent who is online and free. During Open Enrollment, that keeps one team from sitting idle while another watches calls wait.

State and License

A call only helps if the agent can work it. Routing rules can match each consumer's state to agents licensed there, so a caller in Florida does not reach an agent licensed only in Ohio. Licensing rules are set by your organization and applied to every call.

Campaign

Campaign rules keep programs separate. ACA/Marketplace, under-65 (U65) private, short-term, and supplemental calls can follow different routing logic, so each product line reaches the agents and teams built to handle it. Call type, including live inbound and AI-screened, can also shape the path a call takes.

Team

Team structure mirrors how your organization is built. IMOs and FMOs can distribute demand across agencies, downlines, and individual agents while leadership maintains a single view of where calls went. Call centers can apply the same rules within their own teams.

Performance

Routing can also account for performance. Call outcomes and agent activity are tracked, so leadership can direct more volume toward the agents and teams whose results support a campaign's goals. The same data feeds reporting, so decisions can be reviewed afterward.

What Is Health Insurance Call Routing?

Health insurance call routing is the logic that sends each inbound consumer call to the right agent or team. Rules typically weigh agent availability, state licensing, campaign, call type, team structure, and performance. Well-built routing reduces idle time, keeps calls with licensed agents, and gives leadership a record of where every call went.

Open Enrollment Readiness
Starts Before November 1

Open Enrollment typically begins November 1, and each phase asks something different of routing and staffing.

  1. Before Open Enrollment

    Confirm which agents are licensed in which states, set campaign rules for each product line, and test routing paths. Review wallet balances and billing settings so spend is visible from the first call.

  2. Peak Volume

    Watch agent availability and call outcomes as volume builds. Adjust team assignments and routing rules when queues shift.

  3. After Open Enrollment

    Compare outcomes and billing by agent, team, and campaign. Decide which sources and teams carry into special enrollment periods and the rest of the year.

Answers to the Questions
Health Insurance Buyers Ask Most

How do you find leads for health insurance?

Shared internet leads, aged lead files, live transfers, and inbound consumer calls drive health insurance lead generation for agents and agencies. Each channel differs in consumer intent, speed-to-contact, exclusivity, and documentation. AllCalls.io provides inbound and AI-screened calls with availability-based routing for ACA/Marketplace and U65 health products.

How much do health insurance leads cost?

What you pay when you buy health insurance leads depends on call or lead type, product line, language, source, campaign, and volume. Cost per acquisition, which includes agent time and unconverted spend, is a more useful measure than cost per lead. AllCalls.io offers tiered discounts based on volume, and agencies can schedule a free demo to review their traffic and discuss a custom rate.

What are health insurance live transfers?

A health insurance live transfer connects a consumer to a licensed agent during a call that began elsewhere, usually after a screener has qualified the consumer. Inbound health insurance calls start when the consumer dials in directly. AllCalls.io supplies live inbound and AI-screened calls and routes each call based on availability, licensing, and campaign rules. ACA live transfers work the same way for Marketplace health products.

Will I pay for calls that fall short of expectations?

Call qualification, AI screening, recording, and billing transparency let you review what happened on a call and match each charge to it.

Can calls be routed by state and license?

Yes. AllCalls routing rules can account for state and licensing, along with availability, campaign, call type, team structure, and performance, so a consumer's call reaches an agent licensed in that consumer's state. Organizations maintain their own licensing information as part of campaign setup.

Does AI screening create compliance risk, and what about TCPA consent?

AI screening supports intake, transcription, and call summaries within agent workflows, with human oversight and compliance teams in control. Consumer consent under the TCPA affects how demand is sourced, contacted, and recorded, and AllCalls supports consent, recording, and monitoring workflows. Organizations remain responsible for the requirements that apply to their programs.

Is AllCalls only for large insurance organizations?

The platform is built for scale and supports agencies, IMOs, FMOs, and carriers routing demand across large agent networks. Independent agents and small teams can also create an account and access live inbound calls.
