Common Questions About AllCalls.io

How is this different from an insurance lead vendor I've used before?

Most insurance lead generation companies sell files. AllCalls.io delivers live inbound calls from consumers with active intent, not static lead files, and you set the routing and quality rules yourself.

Can this scale across a large agent network?

Routing is built around availability, geography, vertical, and downline structure, so volume distributes across agencies, IMOs, FMOs, and carriers without manual work.

How do I know the calls are compliant?

AI screening and call recording operate with TCPA and consent considerations built in. AllCalls.io doesn't provide legal advice, and organizations should confirm specific requirements with their own counsel.

What am I paying for?

Wallet and billing infrastructure gives leadership real-time visibility into spend, volume, and performance.

Is this a call center or a dialer?

No. AllCalls.io doesn't place outbound calls or staff call handling. It sources, screens, routes, and tracks inbound consumer demand.
