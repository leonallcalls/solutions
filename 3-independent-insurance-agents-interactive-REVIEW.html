What Software Does an Independent Insurance Agent Need for Client Acquisition?

Independent agents need three capabilities working together: a source of live consumer demand, control over which calls arrive and when, and a durable record of every conversation. Software for independent insurance agents typically stops at policy administration or quoting. AllCalls handles all three through availability controls, state and vertical filtering, call recordings, prospect storage, and per-call billing, all within a single platform that runs in your browser or on your phone.

Frequently Asked Questions
From Independent Agents

Is creating an AllCalls account actually free?

Yes. Registration carries no cost, subscription, or monthly minimum. Your costs begin once you add funds and start accepting calls, billed per call at a flat rate.

Can I control how many calls I receive and when?

Yes. Calls reach you only while your availability toggle is on. You can also set daily limits, adjust your state and vertical filters, and switch off entirely at any point without penalty.

Are the calls consumer-initiated?

Yes. Consumers reach out themselves after seeing an ad or landing page, then pass through screening before routing. You pick up a live person who is already asking about coverage.

What happens if I get a wrong number or an instant disconnect?

Buffer periods apply, so a call that ends before the billable threshold carries no charge. For a call that clears the buffer but was clearly something other than a consumer inquiry, send support the call details and the team will review it.

What do I need in place before I can start?

An active insurance license in each state you want calls from, plus carrier appointments for the products you intend to write. Keeping that information up to date within the platform is what allows routing to match you correctly.

Can I use AllCalls on my phone?

Yes. The platform works on desktop and mobile, so you can manage availability, review calls, and check your balance away from your desk.

Does this work if I write multiple verticals?

Yes. ACA, Medicare, life, and final expense route through the same account, filtered by what you are licensed and appointed to sell in each state.

Can I pause or cancel at any time?

Yes. The platform operates without a contract, so you can switch availability off, stop funding your account, or close it whenever you choose.

Do I need a dialer or phone system to use this?

Calls route directly to you through the platform itself. There is no PBX to configure and no dialer license to purchase.

Do I need a separate CRM to manage calls from AllCalls?

Not for acquisition and follow-up. AllCalls logs every inbound call with the prospect's contact details, call audio, transcripts, and your personal notes. You can manage your immediate pipeline right inside the platform, or export prospect details to your existing agency management system if you use one for policy administration.
